Horizon Sweeps National STEM Championship!

Published by: Editorial Board | Category: Academics

Horizon Academy has secured First Place at the National STEM Championship held in Science City this past weekend. Our school's robotics and coding teams competed against 50 other schools from across the country.

The winning entry, a fully autonomous search-and-rescue rover named "Titan V2", scored maximum points in navigating the disaster simulation grid and retrieving objects under a tight time limit.

We extend our congratulations to our student engineers and faculty mentors who put in countless hours preparing for this event!
